Hello, I'm Sally

I am a passionate and experienced singing tutor with a strong background in both performance and music education. I hold a First Class BA (Hons) degree in Popular Music with Recording & Technology from the University of Salford, alongside a High Distinction in Introduction to Music Education using the Kodály Concept for Early Years and Primary learners.

I previously spent four years performing and touring internationally with Michael Flatley’s Lord of the Dance, and continue to tour extensively across the UK, Europe, and the United States in a variety of bands and performances.

Alongside singing, I am also a skilled multi-instrumentalist, playing guitar, piano, and violin. My lessons are warm, encouraging, and tailored to each student—helping build confidence, musicality, and a genuine love of singing in learners of all ages.

The aim of my lessons

• Fun and engaging for young children
• Supportive coaching for teens and adults
• Build confidence and vocal technique
• Friendly with a relaxed approach
• Progress at your own pace

My lessons cover most modern contemporary singing styles including pop, jazz, soul, R&B, and folk. All set in a safe, no-pressure space designed to ease anxiety and keep learning enjoyable. Beginners are always very welcome.
